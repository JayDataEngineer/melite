SFX via moss-sfx. Live-proven pattern.

## Laws
- Describe the SOUND, not the scene: "wet meaty thud with bone crack tail" not "a boar charging".
- ONE event per call. Compound requests ("sword hit and then a scream") = two runs.
- Short captions win: 5-15 words. No narrative filler.
- Iterate by rephrasing the acoustic description, not by piling adjectives.
