# THE LAST SIGNAL — Production Brief

## Story: "The Last Signal" (30 seconds)

### Beat Sheet (4 beats × ~7-8 seconds each)

**Beat 1 — The City (0-7s)**
Wide shot: rain-soaked neon megacity at night.
Holographic ads flicker across wet streets.
Camera slowly pushes in on a figure in a hooded jacket.
Mood: isolation, scale, atmosphere.

**Beat 2 — The Message (7-14s)**
Medium shot: the courier stops at a noodle stall.
A glitching holographic display flickers to life.
Text appears: FINAL DELIVERY — PAYLOAD: MEMORY — DESTINATION: THE EDGE.
The courier looks up, conflicted.
Mood: mystery, obligation.

**Beat 3 — The Chase (14-21s)**
Dynamic tracking shot: courier runs through narrow alley.
Security drones sweep searchlights across walls.
He vaults over obstacles, neon reflecting off his jacket.
Mood: urgency, danger.

**Beat 4 — The Choice (21-28s)**
Close-up: courier reaches a rooftop edge.
The city sprawls below — massive, indifferent.
He holds up a small glowing data chip.
A moment of stillness before the final frame.
Mood: contemplation, resolve.

### Visual Style
Cyberpunk noir — deep blacks, neon blues/pinks/oranges.
Handheld camera movement for action, steady for contemplation.
Rain particles throughout.
Holographic UI elements.

### Technical Specs
Target: 30 seconds at 24fps = 720 frames.
Resolution: 608x352 (draft_02mp for iteration).
6 windows x ~70 frames each with 1s latent chain carry.
10 sampling steps (draft quality).
SLA attention for VRAM efficiency.
